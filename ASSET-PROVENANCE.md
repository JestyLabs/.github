# Asset provenance

The organization profile uses the same transparent lockups shipped by the two
application repositories:

- `assets/branding/jesty_thor_header_lockup.png` — canonical asset from
  `JestyLabs/Jesty-Thor-Fix`.
- `assets/branding/jesty_rp_header_lockup.png` — canonical asset from
  `JestyLabs/Jesty-RP-Charging-Separation`.
- `assets/branding/jesty_labs_lockup.png` — Jesty Labs organization lockup
  selected and supplied by the maintainer.

Branding and visual assets were created or refined with generative-AI
assistance under the maintainer's direction and supervision. The maintainer
selected the final designs, reviewed the transparent exports, and approved
their use. Project-specific licensing and third-party notices remain in each
application repository.
